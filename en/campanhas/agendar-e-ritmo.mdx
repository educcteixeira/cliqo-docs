## What you will learn

The campaign delivery modes and how send pacing protects your reputation on large lists.

## Send now or schedule

On the **Delivery** tab:

- **Send now** — when you confirm on **Review**, the campaign is queued and goes out shortly (the badge shows *Queued* until the send starts).
- **Schedule** — pick a date and time; the campaign shows as *Scheduled* and fires on its own at that time.
- **Smart send** — shows as *Coming soon*; it isn't available yet.

## Send pacing

Pacing spreads the send over a window instead of releasing everything in the same minute:

| Option | How it goes out |
|---|---|
| **Instant** | All at once |
| **1 hour · 4 hours · 8 hours · 24 hours** | Spread evenly across the window |

**When to use it**: large lists, or a base still in [warmup](/en/entregabilidade/aquecimento). Sudden volume spikes are one of the classic triggers for blocks at mailbox providers — spreading the same volume over a few hours delivers the same result without the spike.

Two behaviors worth knowing:

- **Pacing is self-correcting.** If one slice falls behind, the next one compensates — the campaign finishes within the chosen window, with nothing left over.
- **No one receives twice.** Duplicate protection is per recipient, independent of how the send is sliced.

## While the send is running

The campaign report follows along live ("Sending…") and **you can close the screen — sending continues on the server**. If a send is interrupted midway, it resumes automatically from where it stopped ("Resuming automatically…"), and no one receives it twice. If the resume stops progressing for more than 10 minutes — or if the send fails —, the report shows the **Resume sending** button, which opens campaign setup with **Resume pending recipients**: it sends only to the pending recipients.
