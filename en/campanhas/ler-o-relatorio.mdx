## What you will learn

What each section of the report shows and how to investigate from the aggregate number down to the individual contact. Metric definitions live in [Understand the metrics](/en/relatorios/entenda-metricas) — this is the map of the screen.

## The top: campaign numbers

Sent (with delivered), clicks (human, % of delivered), **AMP interactions** (people who acted inside the email) and the **AMP rate** (the fraction who saw the interactive version), placed revenue, conversions, average ticket, opens (estimated ⓘ) and unsubscribes. Next to them, the **render type** shows how many opened AMP vs. HTML.

If **"AMP disabled for this dispatch"** appears, the interactive part failed validation and the send went out in the safety version (HTML) — the notice lists the errors found.

## Dispatch details

Who fired it (a person or a schedule), audience, sender, template, UTMs (with "Copy query string"), duration and the **dispatch preview** — a snapshot of exactly what went out, the way the recipient received it.

## Delivery of the sent

The funnel splits the sent into seven destinations that **always add up to the total**: delivered, soft bounce (temporary rejection), hard bounce (permanent), complaint (marked as spam), suppressed (blocked before going out), pending and failures.

Right below, **Excluded from send** explains who was in the audience and didn't receive, reason by reason: were already blocklisted, had already unsubscribed, hard bounce history, no consent, collision with a recent flow, already received (on a re-send).

## Interactions by block

For interactive emails: each block (wheel, survey, form, carousel…) with unique and total interactions and the most frequent values — which prize came up most, how many spins, how many products selected. This is where interactivity turns into insight.

## Per contact

The table lists every recipient: delivery, opened, clicked, interacted, which version they saw, **purchased**. The filters make investigating fast — only those who purchased, only those who complained, only delivery failures. Bot clicks and machine opens (Apple) appear **flagged**, not hidden.

## Export and act

- **Export CSV** — the campaign summary or the full per-contact list.
- **Send test** — straight from the report, with the `[Test]` prefix, outside the metrics.
- **Resume sending** — for interrupted sends, continues without duplicating.
