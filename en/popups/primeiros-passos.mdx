## What you will learn

How Cliqo pop-ups work, the two prerequisites to go live and the cycle of publishing, updating and taking down.

## How it works

A pop-up is a **piece** published on your store by Cliqo: whoever fills it in becomes a contact in your base, **with the consent on record** (date, source, what they authorized), and can trigger the welcome flow next. Everything is managed from here — after the initial pixel installation, you publish and unpublish pieces **without ever touching the store theme again**.

## The two prerequisites

1. **Pixel installed** — the same script that feeds the browsing segments is what displays the pieces. Installation in Settings › Pixel (Google Tag Manager, your platform's admin panel, or a manual tag — 5 minutes).
2. **Verified brand domain** — the piece is only served on pages of the brand's verified domain. It's a safety lock: nobody publishes your pop-up on a site that isn't yours.

## The gallery

In **Campaigns › Pop-ups**, the templates are organized by objective — capture leads, know the customer, recover sales. Available today:

- **Welcome** — the most used: offer + coupon in exchange for signing up. [Full guide](/en/popups/criar-boas-vindas).
- **Prize wheel** — signing up spins a wheel with prizes and weights you define.

The other gallery templates (quiz, cart rescue, free shipping bar, coupon reminder, recommendation rail) appear marked as being finalized or as depending on the store integration — not publishable yet.

## LGPD and accessibility, by construction

Before publishing, every piece goes through **automatic checks** that cannot be skipped: consent with the **legal name, CNPJ (Brazilian company tax ID) and privacy policy link** filled in; minimum accessibility contrast (AA) between the chosen colors; keyboard and screen-reader navigation; an active frequency cap; and the fixed rule that a **pop-up never appears on checkout** — nobody interrupts a purchase in progress.

## Publish, update, take down

- **Publish to the store** — the piece goes live on the pages and conditions you configured.
- Edited a published piece? **Publish changes** updates what is live.
- The **"Live on your store"** section lists what is active, with the switch to turn it off instantly.

Each piece has its own report: impressions, capture rate, coupons used and — a Cliqo exclusive — **why the pop-up did not appear** to those who didn't see it (already signed up, within the frequency interval, closed it twice, another pop-up in the visit…).
