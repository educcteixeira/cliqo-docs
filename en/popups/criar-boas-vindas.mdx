## What you will learn

How to build, configure and publish the Welcome pop-up — an offer with a coupon in exchange for signing up.

## Before you start

- [Pixel installed and domain verified](/en/popups/primeiros-passos).
- The company's legal name, CNPJ (Brazilian company tax ID) and privacy policy URL at hand — without them the piece won't publish.

In **Campaigns › Pop-ups**, choose **Welcome › Use this template**. The editor has four steps: **Choose · Customise · Behaviour · Review & activate**.

## Step 1 · The offer

- **Headline** — the promise ("Get 10% off your first purchase"). Want to test two versions? Turn on **Test two headlines at once** (headline A/B test): each visitor always sees the same variant, and the report compares the rates.
- **Coupon** — a single shared code, with an **expiry** you choose (from 30 minutes to 72 hours from display). A short expiry creates real urgency.
- **Image** — positioned left, right, top or behind; the screen recommends a lightweight WebP.

## Step 2 · What you ask

- **Email** — required.
- **Mobile/WhatsApp** — optional; it only becomes a WhatsApp contact if the person ticks the WhatsApp consent box, which is **separate** from the email one (both start unchecked — authorization is a choice, not a trap).
- Up to **3 extra typed fields** (text, number, date, yes/no) — e.g. date of birth for the birthday segment.
- **Whoever fills it in joins the list** you choose — and can trigger the welcome flow.

## Step 3 · Behavior

- **When it shows** — *from the 2nd page, after a few seconds* (recommended — captures better than pouncing on arrival), *as soon as they arrive*, or *on exit intent*.
- **How often** — the interval before someone who closed it sees the piece again (e.g. once a week, stopping after two refusals). No bombardment.
- **Where it shows** — include or exclude site paths. **Checkout is always blocked**, as a fixed rule.
- Preview on desktop and mobile — the preview runs the real engine.

## Step 4 · Review and publish

The automatic checks verify contrast, complete LGPD consent, accessibility and display caps. All green → **Publish to the store**. The piece goes live within minutes.

## Result

Each submission becomes a contact with consent on record, receives the coupon on screen and joins the chosen list. The piece's report shows the full funnel — appeared → filled in → became a contact → got the coupon → used the coupon — and the reasons for non-display.
