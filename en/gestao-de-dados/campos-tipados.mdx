Custom fields store what is specific to your business: date of birth, favorite store, size, plan. In Cliqo, every field has a **type** — and the type defines what segments can do with it.

## The six types

| Type | Example | What it unlocks in segments |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | "blue", "downtown store" | equals, contains, starts with… |
| **Number** | 42, 1250 | greater than, less than |
| **Date** | 15/03/1990 | before, after, between — and the **birthday** |
| **Date and time** | 15/03/2026 14:00 | same, with time |
| **Yes/no** | accepted, has the app | is yes / is no |
| **List** | sizes: S, M, L | contains the value |

Without a declared type, every value is treated as **text** — and then a date of birth can't feed a birthday segment, and an amount spent won't accept "greater than". That's why the import runs automatic detection and asks for your review.

## How the type is set

- **On import**: when creating a new field from a column, Cliqo detects the type from the values and shows review notes — ambiguous dates (is 12/05 May 12 or December 5?), postal codes that would lose their leading zero if turned into numbers, yes/no columns with empty cells.
- **In forms** (pop-up and form inside the email): every extra field you create is born with the type you chose.

## Rules that save you headaches

- **Dates** accept the Brazilian format (`15/03/1990`) and the international one (`1990-03-15`), with real calendar validation — 31/02 doesn't pass.
- **A value that doesn't match the type becomes text**, never discarded — you don't lose data because of one nonconforming cell.
- **The field key is normalized** (lowercase, spaces become `_`; accents and symbols are **removed**): "Data de Nascimento" and "data_de_nascimento" are the same field. Prefer keys without accents — "aniversário" becomes `aniversrio`.
- **Different forms add up, they don't overwrite**: a pop-up that saves "size" doesn't erase the "birthday" another form saved on the same contact.

## Where the fields show up

- **Segments** — as the "Customer data" dimension, with the operators of the type.
- **Personalization** — in the editor, Personalize menu › "Custom fields": `{{aniversario|em breve}}`.
- **Contact profile** — in the 360 view, Custom fields section.
