Before any flow message goes out, Cliqo's engine asks one last question: *should this contact receive this, now?* The rules below apply to **all** flows, automatically — they exist to protect the contact from message overload and your brand from burning out its own base.

## Quiet hours

Marketing messages **do not go out between 9 pm and 8 am** (Brasília time). A step that would come due in the middle of the night isn't lost: it **waits** and goes out when the window opens.

## Message budget

Each contact receives **at most 3 marketing messages per 7-day period** — campaigns and flows combined. It's the brake against the classic scenario: someone is in the welcome flow, enters the abandoned cart flow, and still gets the week's campaign. Without the budget, that would be 6 messages in a few days; with it, the excess is held back.

## Anti-collision

After **any** marketing message, the contact gets **24 hours of silence** before the next one. Two automations that fired together don't arrive together — the second waits its turn.

## The exception: utility

**Utility** messages — order confirmation, payment notice, delivery status — are exempt from the budget and from anti-collision: the customer is waiting for them. An urgent, transactional notice (like a Pix payment about to expire) can even break through quiet hours — holding that notice until 8 am would be worse for the customer.

## What this means in practice

- A contact "didn't receive" a flow step? Before suspecting a bug, check the rules: they may be in the 24-hour silence, at the weekly cap, or waiting for 8 am. The live funnel shows who is **waiting now**.
- The rules don't discard messages for no reason: what can be postponed waits; what would lose its meaning (the person left the segment, lost consent) is suppressed with the reason recorded.

## The safety nets

Two extra protections stop the engine before damage is done, and let you know:

- **Enrollment breaker** — if an abnormal flood of contacts enters a flow at once (the typical symptom of a broken integration), the flow pauses **before** sending anything.
- **Volume breaker** — if an account's flow sends explode beyond the usual pattern, everything pauses for investigation.

A flow paused by a breaker is flagged in the list, with the reason — resuming is your decision, never automatic.
