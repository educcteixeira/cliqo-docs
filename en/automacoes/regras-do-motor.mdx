Before any flow message goes out, Cliqo's engine asks one last question: *should this contact receive this, now?* The rules below apply to **all** flows, automatically — they exist to protect the contact from message overload and your brand from burning out its own base.

Quiet hours and the budget have a default that each flow can adjust under **Sending limits**, in the trigger panel inside the editor. The same block holds **Enter once per lifetime**: anyone who has already been through the flow doesn't enter again.

## Quiet hours

By default, marketing messages **do not go out between 9 pm and 8 am**, in your account's time zone (Brasília time). A step that would come due in the middle of the night isn't lost: it **waits** and goes out when the window opens. Under **Quiet hours** you change the flow's window — a start equal to the end turns quiet hours off.

The exception is the first message of a **New Signup** flow that goes out immediately: someone who signs up at 11 pm gets the welcome right away, because they have the store open. The following steps of the same flow respect quiet hours.

## Message budget

By default, a flow step **does not go out if the contact has already received 3 marketing messages in the last 7 days** — campaigns and flows combined. It's the brake against the classic scenario: someone is in the welcome flow, enters the abandoned cart flow, and still gets the week's campaign. Under **Weekly cap** you choose another cap for the flow (1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 10).

The cap applies to flow steps: campaigns count toward it, but are not blocked by it. And the step that hits the cap **does not wait** — it is skipped, and the contact moves on to the next step.

## Anti-collision

After any marketing message — from a campaign or from another flow —, the contact's next flow step waits **24 hours**. Two automations that fired together don't arrive together — the second waits its turn. Steps of the same flow follow the delays you designed. The other way around, an email campaign leaves out anyone who got a flow message in the last 24 hours.

## The exception: utility

The rules above apply to every step you build in the editor: today, all of them count as marketing. **Utility** messages — order confirmation, delivery status — would be exempt from the budget and anti-collision, but they're not available yet: the **Automations › Transactional** screen is marked **Coming soon**.

## What this means in practice

- A contact "didn't receive" a flow step? Before suspecting a bug, check the rules. Anyone in the 24-hour silence or waiting for quiet hours to end **waits** — the live funnel shows who is **waiting now**. Anyone who hit the weekly cap doesn't wait: that step was skipped, and the funnel shows the reason among those who *didn't receive*.
- The rules don't discard messages for no reason: what can be postponed waits; what would lose its meaning (the person left the segment, lost consent) is suppressed with the reason recorded.

## The safety nets

Three extra protections stop the engine before damage is done, and alert the Cliqo team:

- **Enrollment breaker** — if an abnormal flood of contacts enters a flow at once (the typical symptom of a broken integration), the flow pauses **before** sending anything.
- **Age brake** — if a step has been overdue for more than 24 hours (the typical case is reactivating a flow after a long pause), the flow pauses instead of delivering a stale message. Reactivating pauses it again while the backlog exists — write to us at [contato@cliqo.io](mailto:contato@cliqo.io) to decide what to do with those enrollments.
- **Volume breaker** — if an account's flow sends go past the hourly cap, every flow in the account pauses for investigation.

None of them resumes on its own: the flow only sends again once it is reactivated.
