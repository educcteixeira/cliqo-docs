Cliqo is a Brazilian interactive messaging platform for e-commerce. The core idea is a single one: **your customer decides inside the message** — answers a survey, spins a wheel, picks a product, and even pays with Pix without leaving the inbox.

## What you can do with Cliqo

**Interactive email campaigns.** Every email goes out in an interactive version (AMP) for people who open it in Gmail and Yahoo, with an equivalent HTML version generated automatically for every other email client. No one receives a broken email — and you don't have to do anything to make that happen.

**Automations.** Flows that work on their own across email and WhatsApp: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase. You turn on a ready-made template in a few clicks and fine-tune everything on the canvas later.

**WhatsApp.** Conversations, bulk sends with Meta-approved templates, and monitoring of your number's health — on your own WhatsApp Business account, which remains yours.

**Pop-ups on your store.** Contact capture on your site, with consent recorded and the lead landing straight in your base, ready for the welcome flow.

**Reports with revenue.** Each sale is credited once, based on a click or a real interaction — not on an open, which today is an inflated metric. You see how much revenue Cliqo brought in, by channel and by campaign.

## Who it's for

For anyone running e-commerce in Brazil — from a store on Loja Integrada to an operation with a team and an agency — who wants email and WhatsApp to generate measured sales, not just "engagement".
