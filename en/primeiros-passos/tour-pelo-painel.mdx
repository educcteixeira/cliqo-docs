This article mirrors the Cliqo menu, in the order it appears. Use it as a map: every item here becomes a section of this help center.

{/* print: dashboard with the sidebar menu open */}

## Sidebar menu

| Item | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Revenue attributed to Cliqo, engagement funnel, conversions by channel, and trending products. |
| **Campaigns › Email** | Your email campaigns: create, schedule, track, and view reports. |
| **Campaigns › Pop-ups** | The capture pieces published on your store, each with its own report. |
| **WhatsApp › Conversations** | Who replied or messaged you on WhatsApp. The counter shows the conversations **awaiting your reply** within the 24-hour window. |
| **WhatsApp › Messages** | Bulk sending: who receives, what to say, review, and track. |
| **WhatsApp › Templates** | The message templates approved (or under review) at Meta. |
| **WhatsApp › Number & quality** | Your number's Meta rating, the daily limit, and the negative signals. |
| **Automations › Flows** | Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase — and the canvas to build your own. |
| **Automations › Transactional · Cashback** | Pages for the module's upcoming launches — with an availability notice. |
| **Audience › Contacts** | Your base, with the 360 view of each contact and the suppressions. |
| **Audience › Lists & Segments** | Manual lists and dynamic segments (engagement, browsing, RFM). |
| **Audience › Orders** | Store orders and Pix/card charges generated by Cliqo. |
| **Templates** | The interactive email gallery and the templates you saved. |
| **Reports › By campaign** | Aggregated performance, by channel and by campaign, with export. |
| **Reports › Analytics** | RFM matrix, revenue attribution, products, and purchase insights. |
| **Settings** | Account, WhatsApp, sending, brand, domain, pixel, tags, payments, products, integrations, and API. |

Items marked **"Coming soon"** (such as SMS) are not available yet — they appear in the menu so you know what's on the way, but they don't open a screen.

## Top bar

- **New campaign** — opens the starting point: pick an interactive template, create from scratch in the editor, or import a ready-made HTML.
- **Needs attention bell** — flags what needs your attention: sender not configured, DKIM missing, stalled integration, complaints or bounces above the limit.
- **Brand switcher** — appears when the account has more than one brand. Each brand isolates its own domain, WhatsApp number, templates, contacts, and campaigns.
- **Avatar** — Settings, Members, Trash, Audit log, language switch (Português/English), and sign out.
