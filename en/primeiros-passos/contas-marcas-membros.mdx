Three concepts organize everything in Cliqo — and understanding the difference avoids confusion from day one.

## Account and brands

The **account** is the organization — who contracts Cliqo and invites the team. **The brand is what sends**: each brand has its **own, isolated** sending domain, WhatsApp number, logo and colors, templates, contacts, lists, and campaigns.

Every account is born with one brand. If you operate a single brand, you'll never think about this — Cliqo doesn't show the switcher. When you create the second one (name, identifier, domain, and color), the **brand switcher** appears in the top bar, and everything you see and do belongs to the active brand.

**When to use multiple brands**: groups with more than one store, agencies operating clients, or brands under the same owner with separate identities. A contact that exists in both brands is, to Cliqo, two independent records — each with its own consent, which is exactly what the LGPD expects.

## Members and roles

In **Members** (avatar menu), you invite the team by email — the invitation expires in 7 days — and set the role:

| Role | What it can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including transferring ownership and closing the account. |
| **Admin** | Everything day-to-day: members, API keys, tags, trash, audit log, permanent LGPD deletions. |
| **Editor** | Creates, edits, and **sends** campaigns; works with lists, segments, and templates. |
| **Viewer** | View only — ideal for an agency or leadership to follow results with no risk of changing anything. |

Permissions are enforced on the server, not just in the interface — a Viewer can't send a campaign even through a direct API call.

## Traceability and safety

- **Audit log** — administrative actions (invitations, role changes, deletions, API keys) are recorded with author and date.
- **Trash** — deleted lists, templates, campaigns, and contacts stay restorable for 30 days; deleting permanently is an Owner action, with typed confirmation.
