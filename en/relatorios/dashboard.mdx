The Dashboard answers the business owner's question: **how much revenue is Cliqo bringing in — and where is it coming from?** Everything else on the screen exists to explain that number.

## Revenue attributed to Cliqo

The highlight at the top: the revenue credited to your messages in the chosen period, with the return per real invested next to it. Each sale is credited **once**, even if the customer was touched by several campaigns — the rules are in [Understand the metrics](/en/relatorios/entenda-metricas).

Next to it, the **attributed average ticket** compared with the store's overall ticket — a well-segmented message tends to sell a bigger cart, and this is where that shows up.

When there's a sale that Cliqo saw but couldn't credit with certainty (the buyer didn't identify themselves on the site), the screen shows the **"not measured"** notice with the explanation — instead of inflating the number or pretending the sale never happened.

## The month's indicators

Contacts, campaigns, WhatsApp and store sales — each with the change against the previous period and the trend line.

## Engagement funnel

Sent → delivered → opened → clicked → converted, for the period. It's the quick view of where the funnel narrows: low delivery is a list problem; low clicks with high opens is a message problem.

## Conversions by channel

The table that compares **email and WhatsApp** side by side: sends, sales, revenue and average ticket per channel, with each one's share of revenue. Channels not launched yet appear as "coming soon" — no invented rows.

## Product trends

Fed by the pixel: the most visited and clicked products over the last few days, with **Out of stock/Low stock** badges when inventory is tracked — and the **Create campaign** shortcut next to each trending product. It's the short loop: you saw the demand, you built the campaign.
