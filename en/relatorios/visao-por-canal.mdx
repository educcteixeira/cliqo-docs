## What you will learn

How to use the **Reports › By campaign** screen to compare campaigns, channels and periods — and export everything.

## Filters and tabs

At the top: the period (presets or a custom range), the list and the campaign search. The tabs separate the channels:

- **All channels** — the comparable layer: reach, conversions, average ticket and revenue per channel, with the engagement column adapted to each one (opens for email, reads for WhatsApp). What isn't comparable isn't forced: pop-ups, which have no "send", appear with their own columns.
- **Email** — sent, delivery, unique opens (with the Apple Mail caveat), AMP interaction rate, clicks, attributed revenue and CTOR — the click-to-open rate, which judges the message rather than the list.
- **WhatsApp** — sent, delivery, reads, replies, clicks and failures, with the *send-to-reply* funnel and the **"Why it didn't arrive"** section, which translates Meta's error codes into plain language (closed window, per-person limit, spam block…).
- **Pop-up** — captures and live pieces.

## Delivery health

On the Email tab, the three vitals with their alert thresholds: **bounces** (attention above 2%), **complaints** (Gmail considers it problematic above 0.1%… and critical above 0.3%) and **unsubscribes** (attention above 1%). These are the same numbers that feed the Needs attention bell.

## The three lenses

The **View by** selector changes how the table is grouped:

- **Campaign** — each row is a campaign.
- **Flow** — the combined performance of each automation flow.
- **Type (tag)** — groups by [campaign tags](/en/campanhas/criar-e-enviar) (`black-friday`, `lançamento`…). Mind the warning on the screen itself: the tag lens **doesn't sum across rows** — a campaign with two tags appears in both.

## Trend over time

The engagement chart (opens, interactions, clicks) by day, week or month, to see the trend beyond any individual campaign.

## Export

**Export CSV** or **Export Excel** with the applied period and filters — ready for your manager's spreadsheet or the agency's report.
