## What you will learn

How to dispatch a WhatsApp campaign safely: the eligibility funnel, sending in waves, and the guards that pause on their own if something goes wrong.

## Before you start

- A [connected number](/en/whatsapp/conectar-seu-numero) and an [approved template](/en/whatsapp/modelos-de-mensagem).
- An audience with a [registered opt-in](/en/whatsapp/opt-in).

Go to **WhatsApp › Messages**. There are four steps.

## Step 1 · Who receives

Pick lists and segments, like in an email campaign. The screen shows the **eligibility funnel**: from the total selection, how many drop out for having no number, an invalid number, no opt-in (or having blocked you) and repeated phones — down to the final count that **will receive**. Watch for the warning when the chosen segment only looks at email behavior.

## Step 2 · What to say

Two audiences, two costs:

- **Open conversations** (people who replied in the last 24h): get **free-form text, with no template cost**.
- **Everyone else**: gets the **approved template** — these are the messages Meta charges for.

Fill in the template variables (name, coupon, product — with a fallback value for contacts missing the data), set the **button destination** when the template has a tracked link, and send a **test to your own number** — the screen shows the real path (sent to Meta ✓ → delivered ✓) and explains any error.

## Step 3 · Review — waves and guards

The summary shows the audience, message type, **estimated cost** and how much of the number's **daily limit** the send consumes. Choose when to send:

- **In waves (recommended)** — the send goes out in slices: you set the first wave's size and the growth, and the planner spreads it out respecting Meta's daily limit. You can fire each wave manually or let it continue on its own at a set interval.
- **All at once** — only for small, warm audiences.
- **Schedule** — date and time.

And the **guards** ("stop on its own if things go wrong"): automatic pause if blocks pass the threshold you set, pause if the **number's rating drops**, and sending **only between 9am and 8pm** — this last one is fixed by default out of respect for the recipient (the hours are adjustable, the principle is not).

## Step 4 · Track

In real time: sent, failures, held (outside allowed hours or waiting on the limit), **blocks** and the **Meta rating**. States you may see:

- **Held: outside the allowed hours** — resumes on its own inside the window.
- **STOPPED: N people blocked you** or **STOPPED: the number's quality dropped** — a guard fired; the decision to continue is yours, with the data on screen ("See number signals", "Adjust guards").
- **Send the next wave** — when wave mode is manual.

After the first dispatch, the audience is **frozen** — to change the audience, create another campaign.
