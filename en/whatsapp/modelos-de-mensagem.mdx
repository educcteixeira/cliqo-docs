## What you will learn

How to create a WhatsApp template, submit it for Meta's approval and use buttons with click tracking — which is what ties a sale to a message.

## Why approval exists

To start a conversation outside the [24-hour window](/en/whatsapp/primeiros-passos), Meta requires a template it has approved in advance. Approval is usually fast (minutes to a few hours), and the templates stay **in your account** — approved once, used forever.

## Create a template

In **WhatsApp › Templates**, click to create a new template:

1. **Name** — lowercase and underscores (the screen normalizes it for you). E.g. `oferta_semana`.
2. **Category** — Meta charges and judges by it:
   - **Utility** — order, delivery, payment. Cheaper; inside the 24h window, no template cost.
   - **Marketing** — promotion, news, coupon.
   - **Authentication** — access codes.
   Don't try to pass marketing off as utility: Meta reclassifies it and can pause the template.
3. **Message** — the text, with variables `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` for whatever changes per person or per campaign (name, coupon, product).
4. **An example for each variable** — mandatory. **A variable without an example is the most common cause of rejection** — Meta needs to see what the message looks like filled in.
5. **Buttons** — quick reply (e.g. "Tell me more") and/or a **link** button.

## Track the button click

On the link button, turn on **Track clicks on this button**: each recipient gets a unique link, the clicks show up in the report and the **revenue is attributed** to the WhatsApp campaign. With tracking off, the destination is a fixed URL frozen by Meta at approval — no attribution and no changing the destination per campaign.

Tip: include the opt-out instruction ("reply SAIR to stop receiving messages") — the screen offers to insert it with one click when the template doesn't have it.

## Statuses and what to do in each one

The statuses come straight from Meta and appear as Meta reports them:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PENDING** (in review) | Waiting on Meta — usually minutes to hours. |
| **APPROVED** | Ready to use in sends and flows. |
| **REJECTED** | The screen shows the reason; fix and resubmit. Common causes: a variable without an example, text that violates the policy, wrong category. |
| **PAUSED** | Meta paused it for low quality (too many blocks/reports) — review audience and frequency before insisting. |

Deleting a template removes it **on Meta as well** — and breaks campaigns and flows that use it; the screen warns you first.

## Ready-made message gallery

So you don't start from scratch: the gallery brings ready-made templates by use case (welcome, cart, payment notice, post-delivery…), which you personalize with your brand name and submit for approval.
