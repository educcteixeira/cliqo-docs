Meta continuously evaluates every WhatsApp Business number by the experience of the people who **receive**: blocks and reports drive the evaluation down; well-received conversations sustain it. The **WhatsApp › Number & quality** screen brings this data synced from Meta and answers the question that matters: *does Meta still trust this number?*

## The three indicators

- **Meta rating** — **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. It is the quality yardstick. A low rating limits and can pause your marketing sends; Cliqo pauses campaigns automatically when it drops ([guards](/en/whatsapp/enviar-em-massa)).
- **Number status** — connected, restricted or flagged.
- **Daily limit** — how many unique recipients you can initiate with per day. It starts low and rises in tiers (250 → 2,000 → 10,000 → more), with consistent use at a healthy rating and **business verification** completed. The screen shows how much of the limit was used today and the progress toward the next tier.

## Negative signals

Measured over the last 7 days. Mind the difference: what **drives the rating down** is blocks and reports (the spam signal below); opt-outs via SAIR do **not** lower the rating — they are a thermometer telling you the audience or the frequency is wrong, before it turns into blocks:

- **Opt-outs** — people who replied SAIR. Doesn't affect the rating, but it's the early warning.
- **Spam failures** — the most serious signal: Meta blocked delivery after classifying it as spam. This is the signal that pauses campaigns.
- **Per-person limits** — Meta limits how many marketing messages a single user receives from all businesses combined; failures for this reason **are not your fault** and are shown separately.
- **Delivery** — the rate of delivered over sent.

Worth knowing: **Meta does not tell you who blocked or reported** — no tool has that data. What exists are the aggregates above, and Cliqo keeps their **daily history** cross-referenced with the campaigns you sent — that is where the cause of a drop shows up.

## Number identity

The same screen tracks the **display name** (approved or in review at Meta), **business verification** and the **official badge** (the verified WhatsApp account) — with the badge request right there once the prerequisites are met.

## How to keep the rating high

1. Marketing only with a [real opt-in](/en/whatsapp/opt-in).
2. Start with **utility** messages — they build reputation at near-zero risk.
3. Use **waves with guards** on every marketing send.
4. Frequency with common sense: the same contact doesn't need marketing every week.
5. Reply to conversations — a number that only talks and never listens is the classic profile that gets reported.
