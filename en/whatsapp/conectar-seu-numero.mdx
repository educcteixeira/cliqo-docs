## What you will learn

How to connect your company's WhatsApp Business account to Cliqo, through the official Meta API — with the account, the number and the card in your name.

## Before you start

- **Administrator access** to the company's Meta Business Manager — if the company doesn't have one yet, creating it is part of the process.
- **A phone number for the channel** — landline or mobile, able to receive a verification call or SMS. Important: a number that is already active in the WhatsApp app (regular or Business) must be unlinked before it can become an API number.
- **Company details** — legal name, address and website, for Meta's verification.

## Step 1 · Start the connection with Cliqo

The connection is **conducted together with the Cliqo team** (reach us via Settings › WhatsApp or through support): in Meta's signup you log in with your own account and authorize — and the WABA is created (or connected, if it already exists) **in your portfolio**, not in Cliqo's. This guidance exists because the steps on Meta's side change frequently, and a mistake here (a stuck number, a declined name) costs days.

## Step 2 · Register the number

In Meta's flow, enter the number and the **display name** (the brand name your customer will see), and confirm the verification code. The display name goes through a Meta check — names that don't match the public brand can be declined.

## Step 3 · Register the card with Meta

In Meta's WhatsApp Manager, register the **company's payment method**. That card is what pays for conversations — directly to Meta, at Meta's official rates, without going through Cliqo. Without an active payment method, sending stops once the free allowance runs out.

## Step 4 · Verify the company

**Business Verification** confirms the legal name with documents. It is what unlocks the higher sending limits — an unverified number stays on the starting tier of recipients per day. The status shows in [Number & quality](/en/whatsapp/numero-e-qualidade).

## Result

The number shows as connected in Cliqo: **Conversations** starts receiving your customers' messages, **Templates** can be created and submitted to Meta, and the **Number & quality** screen shows the rating, status and daily limit synced from Meta.
