## What you will learn

How WhatsApp works inside Cliqo, what you need before you start, and the path to your first message.

## The model: the account is yours

Cliqo runs WhatsApp through the **official Meta API**, in the model where **everything stays in your name**:

- The WhatsApp Business account (WABA) lives in **your** Meta Business Manager — not Cliqo's.
- The **number is yours**, the display name is your brand's, and the **approved templates are yours**.
- The **credit card is registered directly with Meta**: you pay for conversations at Meta's official rates, with no intermediation and no Cliqo margin. The invoice comes from Meta.

That means two practical things: full cost transparency, and freedom — if you ever switch tools, you take the account, the number and the templates with you.

## What you need to have

- A **Meta Business Manager** for your company, with you as an administrator.
- A **phone number** dedicated to WhatsApp Business — one that is not in use in a regular WhatsApp app.
- **Business verification** with Meta (legal name and documents) — required to raise your sending limits.

The step-by-step is in [How to connect your number](/en/whatsapp/conectar-seu-numero).

## The rules of the game (summary)

WhatsApp is a channel with its own rules, which Cliqo enforces for you:

- **Bulk messaging requires a Meta-approved template** — in the right category (marketing, utility or authentication). [Message templates](/en/whatsapp/modelos-de-mensagem).
- **Marketing requires a registered opt-in**; utility tied to a customer's own order is the exception. [Opt-in and consent](/en/whatsapp/opt-in).
- **When the customer replies, the 24-hour window opens** for free-form conversation — no template and no template cost.
- **Meta gives your number a rating** and a daily sending limit; blocks and reports drive the rating down. Cliqo monitors it and brakes on its own before the damage is done. [Number & quality](/en/whatsapp/numero-e-qualidade).

## The path to your first message

1. [Connect your number](/en/whatsapp/conectar-seu-numero)
2. Make sure the audience that will receive it has an [opt-in](/en/whatsapp/opt-in)
3. [Create and get a template approved](/en/whatsapp/modelos-de-mensagem)
4. [Send in bulk with waves and guards](/en/whatsapp/enviar-em-massa)
5. Reply to the conversations that come back — in **WhatsApp › Conversations**
